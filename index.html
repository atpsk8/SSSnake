How to play

Controls

  • Arrows/WASD or swipe to turn
  • Space, two-finger tap or Shed to shed your tail
  • Hold Shift or Surge to boost
  • P/Esc to pause, Enter to retry

Shed & Surge

Shed drops the back half of your tail. It hardens into a wall for 15 seconds. Longer snakes score more, so shedding trades points for room.

Surge speeds you up and doubles points while it lasts. Eating refills the meter.

Eat again within 2 seconds to build a combo up to x4.

Power-ups

    Hazards

    • Mines: deadly only while lit. Watch the pulse.
    • Rival: steals food. Make it crash into you for a bonus.
    • Mice: run away; catch them for points.
