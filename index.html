      How to play

      Controls

      • Arrows/WASD or swipe to turn
      • Space, two-finger tap or Shed to shed
      • Shift/E, press-and-hold or double-tap for your ability
      • P/Esc pause, Enter retry
      • Versus: P1 WASD Q shed E ability; P2 Arrows / shed . ability

      Coil

      Close a loop with your body (your shed skin counts too). Everything inside is captured: food at double points, mice, mines, even the rival.

      Shed

      Drops the back half of your tail as a wall for 15 seconds. Longer snakes score more, so it's a trade. Bosses fall for it.

      Snake types

        Power-ups

          Hazards

          • Mines: deadly only while lit.
          • Rival: steals food. Coil it or make it crash into you.
          • Mice: run away; catch them for points.
          • Bosses every 5th level. Lure the Serpent King into your shed skin; eat the Swarm Queen's drones to drop her shield.

          Arena twists

            Arcade runs

            Clear a level to draft a mutation. You get one rewind per run, which undoes your last few seconds when you die.
